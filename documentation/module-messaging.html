SMS & Messaging

Reach guardians and staff by SMS: save reusable templates, keep a phone book, send to a whole class or a custom list, and track delivery — all metered against a prepaid SMS balance.

Menu: SMS & Messaging Permission: messaging.manage Typical user: Admin / Office

Templates

Save the messages you send often — fee reminders, absentee notices, result announcements, holiday notices — so staff pick a template instead of retyping. Templates keep wording consistent and correct.

Phone book

A directory of contacts (guardians, staff, custom groups) you can send to directly, in addition to audiences derived from student/class data.

Sending

Choose an audience (a class/section, selected contacts, or a custom list), pick a template or type a message, and send. Before anything goes out, DecentEdu checks your balance.

  1. Pick audience & message
    Class-wise, section-wise, contact-wise or file/list based.
  2. Cost check
    Recipients × unit cost is computed; if the balance is too low the send is blocked (no partial sends).
  3. Deduct & queue
    The balance is decremented and a batch is created; individual messages are queued to the gateway.
💳

Balance guardrail. Every SMS — whether sent manually here or triggered by another module (e.g. absentee notices) — goes through the same balance check, so you can never overspend.

Delivery batches & balance

Each send becomes a batch you can open to see per-message status (queued, sent, delivered, failed). The balance screen shows remaining credit and supports top-ups.

Send→ Balance check→ Batch created→ Gateway delivery→ Status log

Triggered messages

Beyond manual sends, messaging is the channel other modules use for notifications — most notably absentee SMS to guardians and result/fee announcements. The gateway is pluggable, so the same flow works across providers.
