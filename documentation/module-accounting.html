Accounting

A proper double-entry general ledger. Maintain a chart of accounts, post balanced vouchers (receive, payment, contra, journal), and read the school's financial position through a trial balance and income statement. Fee collections post here automatically.

Menu: Accounting Permission: accounting.manage Typical user: Accountant

Chart of accounts (ledgers)

Ledger accounts are the buckets money moves between — Cash, Bank, Tuition Income, Salary Expense, and so on. Each account has a type that determines how it behaves in reports (asset, liability, income, expense, equity).

Vouchers & double-entry

Every financial transaction is a voucher made of entries that debit some accounts and credit others. The golden rule is enforced on save:

⚖️

Debits must equal credits. A voucher whose total debit ≠ total credit is rejected. This keeps the books balanced by construction.

Voucher typeUsed for
ReceiveMoney coming in (e.g. fee collection, donations).
PaymentMoney going out (salaries, bills, purchases).
ContraTransfers between own accounts (cash ↔ bank).
JournalAdjustments and non-cash entries.

Example: a fee receipt

Voucher (Receive) — Receipt #000123
  Debit   Cash .................. 1,500.00
  Credit  Tuition Income ....... 1,500.00
  (debit total 1,500.00 = credit total 1,500.00 ✓)

Fees → ledger integration

When a fee is collected, DecentEdu raises a FeeCollected event and an Accounting listener posts the corresponding receive voucher — so the general ledger is always the single source of financial truth and never drifts from what the office actually collected.

Fee collected→ FeeCollected→ Receive voucher→ Ledger updated

Financial reports

Trial balance

Every account's debit/credit totals — proof the books balance and the basis for statements.

Income statement

Income vs. expense over a period → surplus or deficit.
