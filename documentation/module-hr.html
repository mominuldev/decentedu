HR & Staff

The HR module holds the school's people on the other side of the desk — teachers and staff — along with their designations and departments, and the subject-teacher assignments that connect a teacher to the subjects and classes they teach.

Menu: HR & Staff Permission: hr.manage Typical user: Admin

Setup lists

Designations

Job titles — Head Teacher, Assistant Teacher, Accountant, Peon, etc. Bilingual, sortable.

Departments (HR Sections)

Organizational units — Academic, Administration, Accounts, and so on.

Employees

Each staff member is recorded with their personal details, designation, department, contact information, blood group and photo. The employee list is searchable, and create/edit happens on a dedicated form. Employees are the people who take attendance, teach routine periods and enter exam marks.

Identity
Name & name (Bangla), gender, date of birth, blood group, photo.
Role in school
Designation and department (HR section).
Contact
Mobile, email, address.

Subject-teacher assignments

An employee is linked to what they teach through subject-teacher assignments: a teacher, a subject, and the class config it applies to. These assignments drive:

  • Routines — who is scheduled for a period.
  • Exam mark entry — which teacher is responsible for a subject's marks.
  • Teacher routine printouts — a personal timetable per teacher.
Teacher+ Subject+ Class Config= Assignment

Staff attendance

Employee attendance (including biometric device punches) is handled in the Attendance module, which has a dedicated staff sub-system alongside students.

At the API level

GET/POST/PUT/DELETE  /api/v1/hr/{resource}                 # designations | hr-sections
GET   /api/v1/hr/employees                                 # list
POST  /api/v1/hr/employees                                 # create
GET   /api/v1/hr/employees/{id}                            # show
PUT   /api/v1/hr/employees/{id}                            # update
POST  /api/v1/hr/employees/{id}/assign-subject             # add subject-teacher link
DELETE /api/v1/hr/employees/{id}/subject-assignments/{id}  # remove link
