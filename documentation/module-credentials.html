Credentials & ID

Issue and print the official documents a school produces for its students — transfer certificates, testimonials and certificates — plus ID cards generated from reusable templates.

Menu: Credentials & ID Permission: credentials.manage Typical user: Office / Admin

The documents

Transfer Certificate (TC)

The leaving certificate issued when a student moves on, capturing their record and conduct.

Testimonial

A character/attendance testimonial for a student.

Certificate

Achievement or completion certificates.

Each is created for a student, stored as a record (so re-prints are consistent), and rendered to a branded, printable document with signatures.

Issuing a document

  1. Choose the student
    Pull their details automatically — no re-typing.
  2. Fill the specifics
    Dates, remarks, conduct and any document-specific fields.
  3. Save & print
    The record is stored and rendered to a clean printable page.
🖨️

Issued documents are kept as records, so the office can reprint an exact copy later and there's a history of what was issued to whom.

ID cards & templates

ID cards are produced from templates you design once (layout, logo, fields, colours). Generate cards for a whole class or selection in one go — each populated with the student's photo and details.

Design template→ Select students→ Generate→ Print cards
