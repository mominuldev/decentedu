Fees

The Fees module manages what students owe and pay: a fee structure per class, waivers for individual students, due dates & fines, and receipt collection that automatically posts to the general ledger.

Menu: Fees Permission: fees.manage Typical user: Accountant / Admin

1 · Fee structure

Heads & sub-heads
Heads group fees (Tuition, Exam, Transport); sub-heads are the billable lines within them (January tuition, Half-yearly exam fee…).
Fee config
The payable amount for each sub-head, per class config, per academic year.
Time config
A due date and a flat fine per sub-head, per year.
Waivers
Named discounts (e.g. Merit 50%, Sibling) that reduce a student's payable.

2 · Assess dues

Assessment assigns the structure to students as student fees (the payable lines each student owes). A student's due for a line is:

due = payable_amount − waiver_amount − already_paid  (+ fine, if overdue)

Per-student waiver configs attach a waiver to a student so their payable drops accordingly.

3 · Due dates & fines

If a line has a due date and it passes, a flat fine from the time config applies. The fine is charged once — the first time a payment is made after the due date — and then tracked so it isn't double-charged on later instalments. Before payment, the dues screen shows a projected fine so the desk knows the real amount owing.

Due date passes→ First payment after due→ Flat fine added once

4 · Collect a payment

  1. Open the student's dues
    See every outstanding line with payable, waiver, fine, paid-so-far and the current due.
  2. Enter amounts
    Pay in full or partially against selected sub-heads. Paying more than the due is rejected.
  3. Save & get a receipt
    A receipt (with a sequential receipt number) is created; each line's status becomes paid, partial or stays due.
  4. Ledger posting
    A FeeCollected event raises the matching receive voucher in Accounting.
🧾

Receipts are immutable records. Partial payments are fully supported — a line tracks paid-so-far and remaining due until it's cleared.

GET/POST  /api/v1/fees/configs         POST /api/v1/fees/configs/assess
GET/POST  /api/v1/fees/time-configs                    # due dates + fines
GET/POST/DELETE  /api/v1/fees/waiver-configs           # per-student waivers
GET   /api/v1/fees/students/{id}/dues                  # a student's outstanding lines
GET/POST  /api/v1/fees/collections     GET /api/v1/fees/collections/{id}   # receipts
GET   /api/v1/fees/reports/daily-collection | dues-summary
